Camp Viola Acceptance Letter

May 31 – June 4, 2026

Dear Parents and Caregivers,

Your child has been accepted to Camp Viola for the week of May 31 – June 4, 2026. We appreciate you letting your child come, and we pray that it will be a wonderful experience for everyone involved. Please carefully read the information below regarding this year’s camp.

Children may check in between 5 p.m. and 6 p.m. on May 31. We will not be ready to begin checking children in until 5 p.m. Your child will receive a medical screening by the camp nurse. All children must pass the screening before they go to camp. Children must be lice/nit free in order to attend camp. At registration, children should be accompanied by a parent or guardian. In addition to items listed on the clothing list, parents should also bring a copy of the child’s current insurance card or Medicaid card. No child will be able to stay at camp this year without these copies.

You will be contacted during the week prior to camp to answer any registration questions you may have. If your contact information changes please notify me at (706) 524-8811, or email me at kstringham@fountainchurch.us. Please contact us if you know that your child will be unable to attend camp so that we can notify the next child on the list. All children should be picked up at Camp Viola between 4:00 p.m. and 5:00 p.m. on Thursday, June 4.

Attached to this letter is a list of things that your child will need at Camp Viola. Click the button below to view that list. Space is limited so please only send the items on this list. Do not send a pillow, bed linens, or towels — they are provided. Children are also not allowed to bring cell phones, radios, electronic games, CD players, toys, or money. Any items not on the supply list should be pre-approved by the director, or they will be taken up until the end of camp. Please bring any medications in their original bottles or packaging.

We look forward to seeing you at Camp Viola!

Sincerely,

Kevin Stringham
Camp Director
